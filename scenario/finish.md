## Best Practices Summary

Here are the key security practices we've implemented:

1. **Never commit secrets** to version control
2. **Use environment variables** for secret access
3. **Separate secrets by environment** (dev/staging/prod)
4. **Use secure secret management tools** like Infisical
5. **Regularly scan for exposed secrets** in your codebase
